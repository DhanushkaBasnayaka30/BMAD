- (decision) Customer access: zero auth/login; area selection dropdown persisted in localStorage
- (decision) Contact CTA: opens bottom sheet with optional name input + skip button, deep links to wa.me with prefilled message; zero customer PII stored
- (decision) Provider auth: mobile phone number (E.164 +94) + password; no email required
- (decision) Search result ranking: In-area matches first; Cross-area visiting matches second with explicit label 'Based in X, visits Y'; shop-only cross-area excluded
- (decision) Trust signals: Verified badge, explicit pricing [price type and visiting fee], and work photo previews on cards and profiles
- (decision) Accessibility and layout: Mobile-first, tap targets >= 44px, no hover-only interactions, body text >= 16px to prevent iOS zoom, English launch with i18n headroom for Sinhala and Tamil
- (decision) Visual tone: Utilitarian and ultra-clear. Off-white background, deep navy/slate primary, high contrast. Amber accent for verified badge and trust highlights. Dedicated WhatsApp green reserved exclusively for Contact on WhatsApp CTA. Clean typographic wordmark.
- (decision) First-time landing: Default to Negombo with visible header pill 'Area: Negombo v'. No blocking modal. Stored in localStorage. Direct SEO URLs /{area}. Area switch retains active query and re-executes.
- (decision) Card behavior: Entire card including thumbnails navigates to Provider Profile. Fullscreen photo lightbox exists only within profile. Work photos optional at registration with category placeholder and No photos yet note; ranked lower than photo-complete listings.
- (decision) Zero results: Helpful fallback with nearby town links, query tweak suggestions, and Message FixNear on WhatsApp concierge CTA to Admin. Zero-result queries logged as demand signals.
- (decision) Provider onboarding: 4-step wizard (Step 1: Phone & Password, Step 2: Identity & Shop, Step 3: Services, Mode & Fees, Step 4: Work Photos). Single question group per screen, progress bar, draft auto-save, review summary before submission, and pending approval screen with turnaround expectation.
- (decision) Admin review queue: 1-tap mobile actions for Approve/Reject. Pre-set rejection reasons (Phone unreachable, Photos unclear/not genuine, Outside launch area, Incomplete details) with optional custom note. Provider dashboard displays exact reason for quick correction.
